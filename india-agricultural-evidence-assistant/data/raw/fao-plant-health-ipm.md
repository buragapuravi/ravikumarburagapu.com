# FAO plant health: integrated pest management and monitoring

This curator-written summary is based on the FAO International Year of Plant
Health information page. FAO notes that plant pests and diseases threaten food
production and rural livelihoods, and presents plant-health monitoring and
early warning as tools for preventive and adaptive action.

The page calls for environmentally friendly approaches such as integrated
pest management when addressing plant pests and diseases. It does not provide
a diagnosis, pesticide selection, product label, application method, or dose.
Any pesticide decision must instead be grounded in current, crop- and
location-specific official guidance and the applicable product label.

This source is international awareness and policy context, not an Indian pest
advisory.
