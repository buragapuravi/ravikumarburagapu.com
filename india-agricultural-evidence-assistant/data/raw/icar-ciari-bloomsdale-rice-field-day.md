# ICAR-CIARI rice field day at Bloomsdale Research Farm

This curator-written summary records the topics and participation figures
reported by ICAR-CIARI for a rice field day at Bloomsdale Research Farm,
Chouldari, on 2 September 2026. It is not a full transcript of the source
article and should not be treated as a current farm advisory.

The programme demonstrated rice varieties developed and demonstrated by
ICAR-CIARI and discussed production technologies for island conditions. The
report also describes a natural-farming-based rice-field demonstration by
ICAR-KVK South Andaman, presented in the context of sustainable and
climate-resilient agriculture. It reports 43 participating farmers: 32 women
and 11 men, including participants from North and Middle Andaman.

The source does not name the demonstrated varieties in the article. Consult the
original ICAR report linked in the source metadata for full context.
