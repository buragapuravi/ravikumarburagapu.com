# ICAR-CIARI rice cultivation training in Andaman lowlands

This curator-written summary records the topics reported by ICAR-CIARI for two
farmer training programmes held in Sitapur, Rangat, and Sitanagar, Diglipur, on
25–26 May 2026. It is not a full transcript of the source article and should not
be treated as a current farm advisory.

The report says the programmes covered improved high-yielding rice varieties,
quality seed production, scientific cultivation practices, soil fertility
management, balanced fertilizer use, and mechanization. The article describes
mechanization as a way to reduce labour requirements and improve operational
efficiency. It reports 53 participants across both programmes: 30 men and 23
women.

For detailed context and any current recommendations, consult the original
ICAR-CIARI report linked in the source metadata.
