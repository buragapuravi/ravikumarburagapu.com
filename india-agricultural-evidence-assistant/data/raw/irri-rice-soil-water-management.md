# IRRI rice soil texture and water management

Summary of the International Rice Research Institute (IRRI) Rice Knowledge
Bank water-management page; international context, not India-specific advice.

IRRI reports total seasonal rice water input of about 400 mm in heavy clay
soils with shallow groundwater, compared with more than 2,000 mm in coarse
sandy or loamy soils with deep groundwater. This shows how texture and
groundwater affect water requirements; it is not a yield comparison. IRRI
also describes puddling to reduce water movement, with effects and trade-offs
that depend on soil and production conditions.

The page does not identify a universally "good" or best soil for rice, provide
a local recommendation, or specify optimal soil pH. Consult local extension
and soil-test evidence for field-level decisions.
