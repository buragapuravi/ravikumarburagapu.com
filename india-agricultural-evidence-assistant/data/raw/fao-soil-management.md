# FAO soil management: context and sustainability

This curator-written summary is based on the FAO Soils Portal pages on soil
management and soil conservation. FAO describes soil management as part of
land management, with interventions selected in relation to soil types,
characteristics, and intended land use.

FAO identifies soil degradation symptoms that include declining fertility,
acidity, salinization, alkalization, soil-structure deterioration, erosion,
loss of organic matter, and biodiversity loss. The source describes soil and
water conservation measures as locally applied agronomic, vegetative,
structural, or management practices. It also emphasizes that management
choices should maintain or improve soil functions and the ecosystem services
that soil provides.

This international overview is for research context. It does not diagnose a
field or provide soil-test interpretation, fertilizer rates, or region-specific
recommendations.
