# ICAR field day on pulses in rice fallows in Meghalaya

This curator-written summary records topics reported by the ICAR Research
Complex for the North Eastern Hill Region for a field day at Umiam on 7 March
2017. It is a historical event summary, not current production guidance.

The report describes a programme about pulses in rice-fallow areas and their
potential relevance to food security and soil health. Scientists discussed
no-till pulse production in rice fallows, including lentil and pea, and pulse
production in maize fallows, including French bean and black gram. Farmers
from five Meghalaya villages attended. The report also describes discussions
about varietal selection, sowing time, and fertility management, without
establishing recommendations for present-day use.

This older source is included to test retrieval of historical research and
regional crop context. Consult current local agricultural experts before
applying any production practice.
