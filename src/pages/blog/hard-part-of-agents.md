---
title: "The Hard Part of Agents Isn't the Model"
description: "Getting a model to do something impressive is easy. Running agents safely, reliably, and at scale is where the engineering starts."
pubDate: 2026-10-04
---

A demo can come together in a week. The next six months go to everything around the model: identity, permissions, observability, cost, failures, approvals, and state.

This piece covers where I think the real work is: giving each agent its own identity, replacing blanket human approval with autonomy earned through measurement, treating evals like regression tests, tracing the full trajectory of a task, and deciding what to build and what to buy.
