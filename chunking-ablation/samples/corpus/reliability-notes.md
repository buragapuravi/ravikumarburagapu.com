# Reliability Practice Notes

Service health reviews compare objectives, budgets, incident reports, capacity forecasts, and alert quality. Teams often discuss response times, request failures, retries, and the availability of dependent systems. A dashboard can show several metrics, but operators still need to understand which signal indicates an actionable problem.

During an outage, responders communicate what is known, what remains uncertain, and when the next update will arrive. A clear timeline helps later analysis. Teams can use a review to identify technical changes, missing safeguards, and improvements to response coordination.
